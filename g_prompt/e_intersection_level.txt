Key Principles:

Prioritize the extraction and classification of risks leading up to the first collision.

Strictly restrict to the risk factors in the list above. Factors outside the list go into "Other Relevant Risk Layer".

Extract only factors whose state is "Level 2" or higher (i.e., a deviated, risky state). Factors in "Level 1" (default safe state) are NOT considered risks and should NOT be extracted.

Focus the analysis on observable traffic behavior, vehicle states, and environmental conditions, excluding driver-specific subjective factors.

Ensure the hierarchical structure maintains logical consistency while remaining flexible to accommodate new or unlisted risks (via "Other Relevant Risk Layer").
